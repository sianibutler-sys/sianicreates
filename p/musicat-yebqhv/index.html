What's holding it back

Five gaps stand between Musicat and the partners it deserves. None of them is about the cats.

  1. Ownership on paper

    Brand partners and licensors ask who owns the characters before anything else. Ten years of work with family, friends and Navdeep needs written assignments to the LLC, covering the character designs, the animation and the original sketch.

  2. One version of each cat

    Each cat is described four different ways across your sites and our call, and two cats share the same virtue on the homepage. Kids, writers, animators and sponsors need one source.

  3. One sentence about what Musicat is

    Instagram, YouTube, both websites and the donation page each describe it differently. The donation page calls it a teen-led series with monthly episodes, which conflicts with a 6–11 audience and a six-month gap on YouTube.

  4. A way to get paid

    Ten years of events, contests and content have built proof and goodwill. There's still no package a school, hospital, venue or sponsor can buy at a set price with a clear next step.

  5. Music you own

    Every famous song or lyric needs a license, and estates can say no. Original music from Corey removes that risk and becomes the soundtrack Musicat owns.

A few quick fixes fit in here too: the broken Instagram link, the dead menu items, the passport placeholder and musicattv.com missing from the Instagram bio.
