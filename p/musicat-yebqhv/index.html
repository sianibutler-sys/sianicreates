Accessibility

Automated WCAG 2.2 AA scan of both sites on November 17, plus keyboard and video checks

Working
Harmony Island's homepage, entry and Watch page pass the automated scan. The entry asks before any sound plays, both sites respect reduced-motion settings, the cast cards work with a keyboard and the videos have play controls.
Needs attention
None of the videos hosted on either site have captions, which matters for deaf and hard-of-hearing kids and for anyone watching on mute in a hospital room. The cast page is the best material online

Each cat carries a real struggle. Country Cat manages diabetes, Hip Hop Cat lives with anxiety, Jazzy Cat survived leukemia as a kitten. For kids in the hospitals you already serve, that's representation they rarely see.

Where it's stuck

  1. Ownership on paper

    You have an attorney and a signed agreement with Navdeep. The next step is confirming in writing that the LLC owns the character designs, the animation and the original sketch. Brand partners and licensors ask about that before anything else.

  2. One version of each cat

    Each cat is described four different ways across your sites and our call, and two cats share the same virtue on the homepage. Writers, animators and sponsors need one source.

  3. One sentence about what Musicat is

    Instagram, YouTube, both websites and the donation page each describe it differently. Someone new can't tell in a glance what Musicat is or who it's for.

  4. Offers people can find and buy

    The partnerships page already lays out ways to work with schools, hospitals, destinations and brands. It sits deep in the menu, and none of the offers shows a starting price or a quick way to book, so interest stalls before it turns into revenue.

  5. Everything lives everywhere

    Bibles, decks, footage and contracts sit across drives, links and inboxes. Every pitch starts with digging.

What you get in Phase 1

Nine deliverables in six weeks. Each one has a format you can open, a job it does, and it stays with you.

One-page scorecard

The day-one scorecard

Where Musicat stands at kickoff, on one page. Audience: followers, views and any email list. Money: what past events and projects cost and brought in, and what Musicat spends each month today. Assets: characters, episodes, songs and partners.

Phase 2 has a starting line, so growth shows up in numbers.

Doc + printable PDF

The Musicat character bible

All eight cats plus Annie, Chief, the Don and Vibrato. For each one: personality, what they want, what trips them up, their transform symbol, how they talk and who they clash with. Built from the bibles you already have, approved by you.

Navdeep, writers, sponsors and licensees all work from the same version.

One page + new bios

Musicat in one sentence, and the map

One sentence that says what Musicat is and who it's for. A one-page map of how Musicat TV, the Musiverse, Harmony Island, live events and the club connect. New bios for Instagram, YouTube and both sites.

Anyone who finds Musicat understands it in seconds.

Sheet + attorney memo

Rights and accounts register

Every character, song, video, contract and account in one sheet: who owns it, whose email it's under, what's missing. A second tab lists every promise of credit, backend or ownership made along the way. Plus a one-page memo of questions for your attorney.

You can answer the first question every licensor and brand asks.

Shared drive + brand kit

The Musicat library

Your strongest 25 videos, clips, decks and photos in one shared drive, with a folder system your team can keep using. A brand kit with logos, character renders, colors and fonts. Existing clips that can be cut into short videos are flagged.

When a partner asks for something, it's one link away, and short-form starts with material in hand.

One-page PDF + full proof page

The proof kit

A one-page sheet for schools, hospitals and sponsors with the numbers that matter and one strong image. Behind it, a full proof page on your site: the UMB, Yale and Seacrest case studies, event clips from Baltimore, Long Beach Island and Jamaica, artist collaborations, your advisory board and quotes from educators and hospital staff, including two or three we gather together if needed. The sheet links to the page.

The sheet wins the first 30 seconds, and the page answers everything after.

Offer + flyer + pipeline sheet

A first bookable offer

One of the programs you already offer, such as a school assembly or hospital visit, chosen with you in week 2 and given a clear price. A short flyer and a booking form, sent by you to 10 warm contacts before Phase 1 ends. Those contacts, and every other one that comes up, go into a pipeline sheet with status and last touch.

Real demand, tested with real contacts, and a Phase 2 pipeline that starts with names in it.

Live on both sites

Site fixes

The broken Instagram link, the dead menu items, the passport placeholder, the Instagram bio and the song references that need a license. Plus the accessibility fixes from the scan: labels on icons, contrast, tap sizes and skip links. Captions for the video library are planned in Phase 2.

Closes the gaps a sponsor would notice first, and makes Musicat usable for every kid it serves.
