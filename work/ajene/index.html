The ask.

Ajené Livingston is a community strategist, nonprofit leader and theatre artist. He had a domain and no brand. He needed a look of his own and a site that could hold his services, his speaking and writing, and the people who vouch for him.

What I built.

Brand identity

A serif wordmark with a lagoon blue period, an AL monogram, and a clay, marigold and mahogany palette.

Pages

Services, impact, speaking and writing, written so people across Chester County and Greater Philadelphia can find him.

Testimonials

Past collaborators send their words, photo and logo through a form. He approves each one before it goes live.

Contact

Every message lands in his inbox, and every sender gets a confirmation email with a copy.

Handoff

Domain, hosting, code and data all live in his own accounts, with a plain-language guide for making edits himself.

Built for phones first.

Headshots in the testimonials are framed so faces never get cropped, on any screen.

Behind the scenes.

  1. Someone sends a testimonial

    It lands in his list, he approves it, and it shows up on the pages where it fits.

  2. Someone reaches out

    He gets the message, and they get a confirmation with a copy of what they sent.

  3. The domain move

    Moved off Squarespace, with free email forwarding in place of a monthly email plan.
