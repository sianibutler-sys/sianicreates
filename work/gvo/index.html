The ask.

GVO Sound runs recording, photo and podcast rooms out of 109 Gibralter Ave, plus a clothing line it has sold since 2020 and a steady calendar of events. All of it lived on a Wix site. Owner Cory McGhee needed one place where artists could book a room, buy the merch and hear the work coming out of the studio.

What I built.

Booking

Recording, photo and content rooms book online. The studio gets a text for every new booking.

Shop

The merch store moved from Wix to Square, with checkout and order confirmation emails.

Music

Song previews from studio artists, plus full session videos pulled from YouTube.

Social proof

An Instagram feed that updates itself, with an approval step first, and Google reviews on the homepage.

Events

An events page with a countdown to the next party.

Opening

An intro animation built around the bat in GVO's logo.

Built for phones first.

On phones, Call, Text and Book a Session stay pinned to the bottom of the screen while you scroll.

Behind the scenes.

  1. Someone books a session

    The booking runs through Square, which the studio already uses, and GVO gets a text right away.

  2. Someone fills out a form

    The answers land in a Google Sheet the team already works from.

  3. The move off Wix

    Domain moved to Cloudflare, the store moved to Square, and nine years of Wix sales and order records were saved to GVO's own Google Drive.
