What I built.

Personal links

Every guest gets their own link, and the envelope greets them by name.

The envelope

Tap the wax seal and the envelope opens to reveal the invitation.

RSVP

Yes or no, plus-ones where allowed, allergies and dietary needs, and a photo release.

Privacy

The exact location stays hidden until a guest says yes. Anyone without a personal link sees a private-invitation notice.

Built for phones first.

The envelope fills a phone screen, so opening it feels like opening real mail.

Behind the scenes.

  1. A guest opens their link

    The guest list marks their invitation as opened, so you can see who has looked.

  2. A guest replies

    The answer lands on their own row of the guest list. Nothing to copy, match up or chase.
